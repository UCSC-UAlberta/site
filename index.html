Who we are

A cyber security community on campus.

The Undergraduate Cyber Security Club (UCSC) is the official undergraduate cyber security organization at the University of Alberta. Run by a student executive team, UCSC is building a cyber security community on campus. Our mission is to raise awareness of cyber security, bring more students into the field, and promote Women in Cybersecurity.
